# Refund policy

Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase. To request a refund, reply to the
invoice email with your order number. Approved refunds are returned to the original
payment method within 5-7 business days.

# Double charges

If you were charged twice, send the order number and the last four digits of the card.
Duplicate charges are refunded automatically after verification, usually within 3 business days.
